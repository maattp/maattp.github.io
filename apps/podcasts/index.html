How it works

What happens when you add a show

The change is pushed to the Mac mini within a second or two. For each show it processes the newest episode, and after that every new one (it checks feeds every 15 minutes, or when you tap “Check now”).

For each episode it downloads the audio, finds the ads (MP3 frame headers for inserted ads on NYT shows, “Sponsor” chapters, and a fast transcript read by Claude Haiku), cuts sponsor reads and promos out, and uploads the result here. Hosts’ own plugs stay in. Spots it isn’t sure about stay in too, marked with an “Ad?” chapter.

Storage is capped at 5 GB; the oldest episodes are deleted first to make room. Removing a show deletes its episodes from the feed.
