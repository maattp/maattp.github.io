pilot the real solar system

Look around by dragging. Pinch to zoom all the way out to the map and back.

Tap a name to target a planet or moon. Your predicted path is drawn ahead of you, with the closest approach to your target marked. Bend the path until it wraps around the target.

Hold BURN to fire the engine. The buttons on the left choose where the nose points: PRO along your motion (speeds you up, raises the far side of your orbit), RET against it (slows you down, capture at a planet), NRM/ANM tilt your orbit, CAM aims where the camera looks.

Warp time with ‹ ›. Warp drops when you burn and near a sphere-of-influence change.

A trip: burn PRO from Earth orbit until your path escapes, keep burning until the closest approach to the target shrinks, then warp there. On arrival burn RET at closest approach until the path closes into an orbit.

Refuel by holding a stable orbit around any body.
