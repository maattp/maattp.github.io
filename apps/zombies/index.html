Controls

Play in landscape.

Touch — drag the left half to move (push forward to sprint), drag the right half to look. FIRE can be dragged to aim while shooting. Tap BUY when a prompt appears; hold it at a broken window to rebuild the boards.

Controller — left stick move, right stick look, RT fire, LT aim, hold L3 sprint, R3 melee, A buy / repair, X reload, RB frag, Y swap weapon, Start pause. In menus: stick or d-pad moves the highlight, A confirm, B back.

Keyboard — WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse look & fire, R reload, V knife, G frag, Q swap, F buy, Esc pause.
